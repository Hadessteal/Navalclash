NavyCraft M5G Hotfix 6 — E Inventory Key
=============================================

Includes Hotfixes 1–5.

Change:
- Press E to open the Minecraft-style NavyCraft inventory.
- The action triggers once per key press, so holding E will not repeatedly
  reopen the screen.
- The normal Luanti inventory key and /nc_inventory remain available as
  backups.

Technical note:
Luanti maps E to the Aux1 control by default. NavyCraft listens for the rising
edge of Aux1 and opens the custom inventory.

Installation:
1. Close Luanti completely.
2. Replace the current games\navycraft folder.
3. Extract this ZIP directly into the Luanti games folder.
4. Confirm games\navycraft\game.conf exists.
5. Restart Luanti.
