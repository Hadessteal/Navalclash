NavyCraft M5G Hotfix 3 — Moving-Deck Death-Loop Fix
========================================================

Includes Hotfixes 1 and 2.

Fixes:
- Stops the Lua passenger teleporter when the native moving-construct renderer
  and rider physics are active. The two systems were carrying players twice.
- Clears remembered Lua passenger anchors on death, respawn, join and leave.
- Prevents downward velocity from accumulating while supported by a moving
  deck.
- Cancels only false engine fall damage while the player is supported by a
  NavyCraft construct. Weapon, drowning and ordinary off-ship fall damage are
  not disabled.
- Automatically performs a short three-stage rider escape after a ship-deck
  death so stale native contact cannot pull the respawned player back.
- Adds /nc_escape for manual rider-state recovery.
- Fixes the Hotfix 2 HUD size vector warnings.
- Moves construct entity static_save into initial_properties.

Installation:
1. Close Luanti completely.
2. Delete or rename the existing games\navycraft folder.
3. Extract this ZIP directly into the Luanti games folder.
4. Confirm games\navycraft\game.conf exists.
5. Restart Luanti before loading the world.

Your existing world may remain in place.
